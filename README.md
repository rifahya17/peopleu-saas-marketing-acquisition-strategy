# 🚀 PeopleU SaaS — Marketing Acquisition Strategy

> **From Lead Volume to Qualified Growth**

An end-to-end **B2B SaaS marketing analytics project** focused on identifying high-value lead segments, evaluating **channel & intent effectiveness**, and translating acquisition data into **incremental marketing investment recommendations**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

PeopleU is already generating leads, but **lead volume alone does not guarantee commercial quality**.

This analysis addresses three core questions:

- 👥 **WHO** are the highest-value lead profiles?
- 📣 **WHERE** should PeopleU acquire them?
- 🎯 **HOW** can intent be used to improve acquisition quality?

### 🔑 Decision Framework

**Audience × Channel × Intent**

The goal is to optimize the combination — **not the channel in isolation**.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 📈 Lead Generation Works
- **3,397** leads analyzed
- **901** contracted leads
- **26.52%** lead-to-contract conversion

### 🎯 High-Value Audiences
The strongest observed acquisition audiences were:

- **251+ Technology**
- **251+ Finance**

### 🔥 Core Acquisition Motions
The strongest observed combinations were:

- **251+ Technology × Referral × Set a Meeting**
- **251+ Finance × Referral × Set a Meeting**

### 📣 Channel Roles Differ
- **CPC** → Volume / efficiency engine
- **Referral** → Quality / high-value enterprise engine
- **Direct** → Strong downstream response
- **Organic Search** → Selective optimization
- **Organic Social** → Reassess before adding spend

### 💰 Investment Insight
Historical spend-response analysis does **not** support a blanket assumption that:

> **+10% marketing spend → +25% leads**

Instead, incremental investment should be released progressively using **evidence-based guardrails**.
