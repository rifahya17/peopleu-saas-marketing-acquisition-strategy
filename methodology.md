# Methodology

## Grain

Lead-level analysis:
1 row = 1 leads_id

Customer 360:
1 row = 1 leads_id

## Qualified Lead

Qualified lead quality combines:

- conversion
- initial economic value
- lifecycle economic value
- renewal
- evidence strength

## Intent

Intent is inferred from pre-registration Google Analytics behavior:

- Brochure
- Demo
- Set a Meeting

## Marketing Spend

Marketing spend is analyzed at monthly channel level.

## Elasticity

Observed elasticity:

% change in output / % change in spend

This is observational and not causal.

## Scenario

Incremental Leads =
Baseline Leads × Spend Growth × Observed Lead Elasticity × Scenario Factor

Scenario factors:

- Conservative = 50%
- Base = 100%
- Upside = 125%
